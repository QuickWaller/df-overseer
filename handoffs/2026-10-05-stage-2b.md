## Result

### Plan (2026-10-05)

Build, all in `df-overseer-blueprint.lua` (plus one stored field in the
reservation record, no change to `df-overseer-reservations.lua` needed since
`create` keeps unknown keys):

1. `status SITE_ID [PHASE]`: adds `phase: {label, mode, applied, leaves,
   cells, buildings, zone_required, zone_present, unread_modes, done}`
   over that phase's own leaves (`leaf_sections`). Buildings: contiguous
   same-key cells of a build/place leaf are one expected building; complete
   means every tile has a building at `getBuildStage() >= getMaxBuildStage()`.
   Zone present means every zone-leaf cell has a civzone (`findCivzonesAt`).
   Without PHASE the output is today's, unchanged.
2. `level` in reserve output (top level) and apply output (`site.level`),
   stored on the reservation and site record; `reservation` in `sites` rows.
3. Reserve (dry and real, also on refusal) returns `finish_plan`.
4. `release SITE [DRY_RUN] [ANY_PENDING]`.
5. Fixtures under `dfmcp/tests/fixtures/blueprint/` from a generator that
   runs the real Lua in lupa, plus a drift test; tests per section 6.2.

Interfaces for 2C are in the Result section once built.

Design concerns found while reading (reported, not silently changed):
- `release ... ANY_PENDING` can only safely undo dig-mode phases offline;
  planned-but-unbuilt buildings and zones are not withdrawn (quickfort `undo`
  semantics for build and zone are unverified here). Reported as
  `skipped_phases`.
- Releasing forgets the site record, so a repeated cleanup call returns a
  `no site` error rather than a no-op.

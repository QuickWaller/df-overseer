# Decision Register

Log every decision worth remembering here: **date**, **status**, **reason**.

Before proposing or researching an approach, check this register first — don't
re-suggest something already tried and rejected without saying so. It's fine to
resurface a rejected approach if the prior rejection may have been wrong or
there's no viable alternative, but say explicitly that it was tried before and
why it's worth revisiting.

| 2026-08-25 | **Replaced the evidence counter.** Hypotheses are weighted by *diagnosticity* (Van Evera hoop / smoking-gun tests), stratified by recorded covariates, with promotion thresholds derived from a stated error rate (Wald SPRT) rather than an arbitrary count. | 2026-08-25 | **Deliberate experimentation promoted to v1**, from "probably not v1" | accepted | Passive learning at N≈20 is close to unworkable — twenty forts in twenty worlds are twenty confounded anecdotes. | 2026-08-25 | **Doctrine size is a hard, measured budget, not a style preference** | accepted | A 2026 controlled study (arXiv:2607.19257, five models incl. Claude Sonnet 5, four formats, both placements, 960 calls/model) found perfect-response rate collapses to zero by **N=80 simultaneous rules**. *Single study, threshold unreplicated* — treat as order-of-magnitude and measure our own compliance-vs-doctrine-size curve early. |
| 2026-08-25 | **The overseer is not a long-running conversation.** Each turn assembles a fresh context from memory; continuity lives in the memory stores, not in message history | accepted | Microsoft/Salesforce (arXiv:2505.06120) measured ~39% accuracy loss as conversations extend. A month-long fortress session naively implemented is one enormous multi-turn conversation. Also rules out the "resident session" loop shape floated earlier. Pairs with "lost in the middle": doctrine goes at the front of context (possibly duplicated at the end), never a middle tier — making the cache-ordering rule a *correctness* requirement, not just cost. |
| 2026-08-25 | **All learning grading is mechanical**, never the agent's self-report; plus an automated auditor cross-checking cited doctrine against actual actions | accepted | A model can produce a compelling account of why doctrine changed its decision without that doctrine having influenced anything (unfaithful-CoT research). Removing the model from the grading loop is the only defence with evidence behind it. Watch-for heuristic: doctrine that only ever grows and is never walked back across 20 forts signals confirmation, not learning. |

| 2026-08-25 | **Deliberate divergence from Letta/MemGPT: the model does not edit its own memory.** Promotion is mechanical, grading is code-driven | accepted | Letta's defining feature is LLM-managed memory via tool calls; we do the opposite.

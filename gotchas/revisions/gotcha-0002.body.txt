Manager work orders DO run (proven live 2026-10-08). If an order sits queued and never runs, check these in order. 1. Material: an item order with "unknown material" (mat -1/-1) never activates; orders.list flags it, so recreate it with a MATERIAL (orders.create now defaults one per job type). 2. Appointment: the manager must be appointed through the game's own route (nobles.appoint, fixed 2026-10-08; an appointment made by the old tool lacked the game's cached indexes, so nobles.verify read MANAGER while the Work Orders screen still said assign a manager). Confirm with nobles.requirements. 3. Office: at 24 citizens the manager must own an office of their own or nothing validates (measured: 19,000 ticks with nothing, validated about 3,600 ticks after the office was assigned). Use zone.assign-owner on the manager's office. Do not read validated=true, active=false as "validation works, dispatch fails": a validated flag with no active flag only means the order is waiting on the checks above, not that the route is broken.
